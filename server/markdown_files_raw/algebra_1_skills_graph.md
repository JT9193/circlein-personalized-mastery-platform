# 1. Foundational Readiness Nodes

## A. Number sense and arithmetic fluency

### F-A1. Read and write integers
- Identify positive, negative, and zero.
- Compare and order integers.
- Depends on: none

### F-A2. Add and subtract integers
- Model integer addition and subtraction.
- Apply sign rules accurately.
- Depends on: F-A1

### F-A3. Multiply and divide integers
- Apply sign rules for multiplication and division.
- Depends on: F-A1

### F-A4. Understand fractions as numbers
- Interpret numerator and denominator.
- Place fractions on a number line.
- Depends on: none

### F-A5. Generate equivalent fractions
- Simplify fractions.
- Create common denominators.
- Depends on: F-A4

### F-A6. Add and subtract fractions
- With like denominators.
- With unlike denominators.
- Depends on: F-A5

### F-A7. Multiply and divide fractions
- Multiply fractions and mixed numbers.
- Divide by fractions using reciprocal reasoning.
- Depends on: F-A5

### F-A8. Convert among fractions, decimals, and percents
- Convert in all directions.
- Interpret percent as “per hundred.”
- Depends on: F-A4, F-A5

### F-A9. Use order of operations
- Evaluate numeric expressions with parentheses, exponents, multiplication/division, addition/subtraction.
- Depends on: F-A2, F-A3, F-A6, F-A7

### F-A10. Estimate and judge reasonableness
- Use benchmarks and mental math.
- Detect implausible answers.
- Depends on: F-A2 through F-A9

## B. Ratio, rate, and proportional reasoning

### F-B1. Understand ratios
- Describe ratio relationships verbally and symbolically.
- Depends on: F-A4, F-A8

### F-B2. Use unit rates
- Compute unit price, speed, and other per-1 quantities.
- Depends on: F-B1, F-A7

### F-B3. Recognize proportional relationships
- Identify constant multiplicative change.
- Depends on: F-B1, F-B2

### F-B4. Solve proportions
- Use tables, scaling, equations, and cross multiplication conceptually.
- Depends on: F-B3

### F-B5. Interpret percent increase and decrease
- Solve discount, tax, markup, and growth problems.
- Depends on: F-A8, F-B3

## C. Early algebraic thinking

### F-C1. Interpret variables as unknowns
- Recognize letters standing for numbers.
- Depends on: F-A9

### F-C2. Translate verbal phrases into expressions
- Example: “three less than twice a number.”
- Depends on: F-C1

### F-C3. Evaluate expressions with substitution
- Replace variables with values.
- Depends on: F-C1, F-A9

### F-C4. Use the distributive property numerically
- Expand and factor simple numeric forms.
- Depends on: F-A9

### F-C5. Combine like terms conceptually
- Recognize same units / same variable parts.
- Depends on: F-C1, F-C2

### F-C6. Solve one-step equations
- Using inverse operations.
- Depends on: F-C1, F-C3

### F-C7. Solve one-step inequalities
- Interpret solution sets on a number line.
- Depends on: F-C6, F-A1

## D. Coordinate plane and graph readiness

### F-D1. Locate points in the coordinate plane
- Plot ordered pairs.
- Identify quadrants and axes.
- Depends on: F-A1

### F-D2. Interpret x- and y-values in context
- Recognize independent vs dependent quantity informally.
- Depends on: F-D1

### F-D3. Recognize patterns in tables
- Identify additive and multiplicative patterns.
- Depends on: F-B3, F-D2

### E-A4. Combine like terms

### E-B4. Represent real situations with expressions

### E-B5. Interpret units in expressions

## C. Exponent foundations

# 10. Mathematical Practices Embedded Across the Graph

These are not separate content strands, but they should be attached to many skills above.

### M1. Make sense of problems
- Identify givens, unknowns, constraints.

### M2. Persevere in multi-step reasoning
- Sustain work through several transformations.

### M3. Reason quantitatively
- Interpret numbers, units, and relationships in context.

### M4. Construct and critique reasoning
- Explain why steps are valid.

### M5. Use representations strategically
- Move among words, tables, graphs, and equations.

### M6. Attend to precision
- Correct notation, labels, units, and arithmetic.

### M7. Look for structure
- Notice repeated forms, patterns, and useful rewrites.

### M8. Look for regularity in repeated reasoning
